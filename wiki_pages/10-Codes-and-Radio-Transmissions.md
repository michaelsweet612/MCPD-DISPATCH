## 📸 Image Analyzer Attachment System
Dispatchers can click the **`+` Upload Button** in the chat bar to attach images from their local computer:
- The offline `imageAnalyzer.js` system analyzes filenames for keywords (e.g. *dog, suspect, nsfw*).
- Patrol units transmit procedural, unhinged reactions across the secure channel based on the image content.

## 📻 Transmissions Engine
Driven by `transmissions.js`, officers feature hundreds of dynamic radio voice lines ranging from standard callouts to chaotic existential dread (e.g. *"Dispatch, I'm eating the evidence. It tastes like purple."*, *"I just arrested a stop sign for resisting"*).
