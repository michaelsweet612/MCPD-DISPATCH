# Rules of Engagement & Emergencies

## Lethal Force Authorization
Officers on patrol will frequently encounter situations that require lethal force. Due to recent budget cuts regarding ammunition tracking, officers may lock your terminal and request explicit authorization to execute a suspect.
- You have **20 seconds** to respond.
- Clicking **AUTHORIZE** allows the execution.
- Clicking **DENY** forces a non-lethal resolution.
- Ignoring the prompt will result in the officer taking matters into their own hands (usually violently).

## 10-99 Officer Panic
When an officer is pinned down by overwhelming firepower, a **10-99 Officer Panic** is triggered.
- The terminal will flash red and an alarm will sound.
- Panics must be manually resolved by the dispatcher, or they will eventually result in officer casualties.

## Code 5 SWAT Deployment
In the event of an uncontrollable riot, anomaly, or Keter-level threat, the Dispatcher may deploy SWAT.
- Click **DEPLOY SWAT (CODE 5)** in the Critical Emergencies tab.
- SWAT deployment instantly pacifies all active panics with extreme prejudice.
- Use sparingly, as SWAT teams tend to destroy a lot of public infrastructure.
