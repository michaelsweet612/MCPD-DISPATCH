# 🧠 Psychological Profiles

Every officer in the MCPD has a unique psychological profile assigned during CAD initialization. This profile dictates their radio chatter, willingness to escalate violence, and how they handle citizen interactions.

---

### 1. 🩸 Aggressive
- **Behavior:** Shoot first, ask questions never.
- **Traits:** High probability of requesting Lethal Force authorization; frequently brags about body counts and firearm caliber.

### 2. 🐾 Furry (10% Spawn Chance)
- **Behavior:** Completely unhinged, cute, and deeply concerning.
- **Traits:** Ignores standard police radio protocol to spam the comms with *"ooh I'm a little furry i'm better than you guys... deserve all the head rubs UwU"* and *"uwu oh I think he's shooting at me! :3"*.

### 3. 💼 Corrupt
- **Behavior:** Motivated purely by bribe credits.
- **Traits:** Files suspicious incident reports claiming perps "escaped" while pocketing illicit contraband.

### 4. 🐣 Rookie
- **Behavior:** Nervous, inexperienced, and terrified.
- **Traits:** Highly prone to triggering 10-99 panics over minor noises or stray animals.

### 5. ☕ Lazy
- **Behavior:** Will actively dodge dispatch orders.
- **Traits:** Prefers sitting in cruisers eating donuts; complains vocally over the radio if assigned high-priority callouts.

### 6. 🎖️ Veteran
- **Behavior:** Cynical, stoic, and unbothered.
- **Traits:** Handles gang shootouts and cosmic anomalies with bored professionalism.

### 7. 📖 By-The-Book
- **Behavior:** Strict adherence to MCPD penal code.
- **Traits:** Reprimands fellow officers for off-topic banter on emergency channels.

### 8. 💣 Reckless
- **Behavior:** High-speed pursuits and excessive collateral damage.
- **Traits:** Solves traffic stops by ramming cruisers into civilian storefronts.

### 9. 🕊️ Idealistic
- **Behavior:** Mistakenly believes in community policing.
- **Traits:** Rarely flags citizens as suspicious; doomed to be disillusioned by the department.

### 10. 👁️ Paranoid
- **Behavior:** Convinced that shadow entities and civilians are plotting against them.
- **Traits:** Draws service weapons on garbage cans, streetlamps, and weather anomalies.
