# Psychological Profiles

Every officer in the MCPD has a unique psychological profile that dictates their behavior on the radio and how they resolve crimes.

### 1. Aggressive
Shoot first, ask questions never. These officers are highly likely to request lethal force authorization and will often brag about their kill counts.

### 2. Rookie
Nervous, inexperienced, and terrified. Rookies are highly likely to trigger 10-99 panics over minor incidents, like loud noises or aggressive stray dogs.

### 3. Veteran
Calm, cynical, and tired. Veterans have seen it all. They handle anomalies and shootouts with bored professionalism.

### 4. Corrupt
Always looking for a bribe. Corrupt officers will frequently file incident reports admitting to letting suspects go in exchange for credits. They are a massive liability to the department's public image but bring in extra budget funding.

### 5. Lazy
Will actively avoid responding to priority calls. Lazy officers prefer to sit in their cruisers eating donuts and will complain over the radio if you transmit orders directly to them.
