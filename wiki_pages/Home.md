## 📖 About the MCPD
The MCPD is a highly fictionalized, dystopian, and trigger-happy police force tasked with maintaining "order" in a sprawling cyberpunk megacity. Operating under **Directive 4-A**, all citizens are presumed guilty until proven otherwise, and officers are encouraged to resolve municipal disputes with overwhelming firepower.
