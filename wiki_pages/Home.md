# Welcome to the MCPD Dispatch Wiki

Welcome to the official internal documentation for the Main City Police Department (MCPD) Dispatch Terminal. 

## About the MCPD
The MCPD is a highly fictionalized, dystopian, and trigger-happy police force tasked with maintaining "order" in a sprawling cyberpunk megacity. Our motto is simple: **Protect and Kill**. (Originally "Protect the Government and Kill Civilians", but PR shortened it).
