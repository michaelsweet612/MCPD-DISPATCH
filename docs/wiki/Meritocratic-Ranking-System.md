# 🎖️ Meritocratic Ranking System

Unlike traditional police departments, the MCPD does not promote based on written exams, leadership seminars, or time served. We promote based on **results**. Specifically, lifetime civilian pacifications (kills).

All officers are instantiated as **Privates** with 0 kills. As they violently resolve crimes, they climb the following 11-tier ladder:

| Rank | Abbreviation | Kills Required | Operational Authority |
|------|--------------|----------------|-----------------------|
| **Private** | PVT | 0 | Standard patrol, baton & basic sidearm |
| **Corporal** | CPL | 250,000 | Authorized shotgun deployment |
| **Sergeant** | SGT | 1,000,000 | Authorized tactical submachine guns |
| **Staff Surgeon** | SSG | 5,000,000 | Field medical & cybernetic harvesting |
| **Sergeant First Class** | SFC | 8,000,000 | Heavy crowd control weaponry |
| **First Sergeant** | 1SG | 9,000,000 | Tactical vehicle commandeering |
| **Sergeant Major** | SGM | 10,000,000 | District-wide curfew enforcement |
| **Command Sergeant Major** | CSM | 35,000,000 | Autonomous drone strike authorization |
| **Sergeant Major of the Police** | SMP | 3,000,000,000 | Orbital kinetic strike clearance |
| **General of the Police** | GEN | 80,000,000,000,000,000,000 | Sector glassing protocol |
| **Chief** | CHIEF | 200,000,000,000,000,000,000 | Absolute totalitarian jurisdiction |

> *Note: Achieving the rank of Chief automatically designates you as the most aggressive entity in the precinct.*
