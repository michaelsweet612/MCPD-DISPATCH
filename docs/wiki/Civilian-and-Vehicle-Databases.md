# 🗄️ Civilian & Vehicle Databases

## 👥 Megacity Citizen Registry
The MCPD indexes over **5,000 active citizens** across the city sectors. The Citizens tab uses **lazy-loaded pagination** — only 10 citizens render at a time, with a **LOAD MORE** button to load 10 more at a time for zero lag.

- **Civilian Number (`#CIV-XXXXXXXX`):** Unique 8-digit government tracking number.
- **National ID (`CID-XXXXXX`):** Municipal biometric identification code.
- **Municipal Standing:** Categorized as `Innocent`, `Suspicious`, `Wanted`, `Arrested`, `Escaped`, or `Deceased`.
- **Gender Identity:** Explicit tracking including `Human`, `Seps`, `Over The Gone`, `Unidentified Species`, and the ultra-rare **1% chance** `Unidentified Gender`.
- **Marital Status:** Tracked as `Single`, `Married`, `Divorced`, `Widowed`, `Married (Corporate Arranged)`, `Legally Separated`, or `Complicated`.
- **Net Worth (NTND):** Every citizen has a procedurally generated net worth in the official **TBMG Trust Nation (NTND)** currency. Distribution: 2% ultra-rich (100M+), 8% wealthy (10M-99M), 30% middle class (1M-9M), 35% working class (100K-999K), 25% poverty. Rich citizens (10M+) are highlighted in green.
- **Status / Occupation:** Citizens are assigned procedurally generated job titles from over **136,000+ combinations** (e.g. "Corporate Data Analyst", "Synthetic Spaghetti Wrangler", "Black-Market Biomass Scrapper"). 15% chance of being `Unemployed`.
- **Terminal IP (TBMG Grid):** Procedurally generated zero-padded terminal IPs (e.g. `005.412.091.992`). All Geo-Trace offshore to the **Gulf of TBMG**.
- **Execution Licenses:** Citizens can hold up to **58 different classes**, ranging from Class 1 all the way up to the terrifying **Class 115 Total Annihilation Execution Permit**.
- **Dossier Management:** Dispatchers can inspect criminal records, cybernetic implants, residential sectors, and execute warrants.
