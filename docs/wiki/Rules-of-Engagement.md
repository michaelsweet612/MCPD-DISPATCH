# ⚠️ Rules of Engagement & Emergencies

## 🎯 Lethal Force Authorization
Officers on patrol will frequently encounter non-compliant citizens or high-threat targets. Due to ammunition audits, officers will occasionally lock your dispatch terminal and request explicit authorization to execute a citizen.
- **20-Second Countdown:** The dispatcher has exactly 20 seconds to choose **AUTHORIZE (YES)** or **DENY (NO)**.
- **Authorize (YES):** The officer engages with lethal force. However, **Internal Affairs (E.I.)** may intercept the radio to reprimand you for excessive brutality.
- **Deny (NO):** The officer begrudgingly stands down and complies with standard procedures.
- **Timeout Failure:** If you fail to respond in 20 seconds, the rogue officer will randomly decide whether or not to open fire.

---

## 🚨 10-99 Officer Panic
When an officer is overwhelmed by hostile firepower:
- A flashing red visual alarm triggers and the procedural panic siren wails.
- The unit is marked as **CRITICAL / PANIC**.
- Dispatchers must deploy backup or resolve panics to prevent officer injury or casualties.

---

## 🛡️ Shift Management (54/4 Rule)
The MCPD maintains a strict shift quota:
- **54 Officers On-Duty / 4 Officers Off-Duty**.
- Shifts dynamically rotate every 60 seconds.
- Off-duty officers occasionally sustain injuries during their time off, requiring medical triage upon clocking back in.

---

## 🚁 Code 5 SWAT Deployment
In the event of civil insurrection or multi-block unrest:
- Click **DEPLOY SWAT (CODE 5)** in the Critical Emergencies menu.
- Deploys heavy armored tactical units to pacify all active panics instantly.
