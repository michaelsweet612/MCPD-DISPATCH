📖 MCPD DISPATCH OPERATOR MANUAL

Welcome to the MCPD Dispatch System

Welcome, Operator. You are currently logged into the Metro City Police Department Central Dispatch Terminal (v4.21).

This system acts as the digital backbone of the entire police force. From this seat, you will monitor all 5,000 active officers, coordinate responses to cyber-crimes, handle internal communications, process IT support requests, and approve new recruits.

Use the sidebar on the left to navigate this manual and understand the specific subsystems you are responsible for.

The Main Terminal (Unified Log)

The Unified Log is the massive central chat window in the middle of your screen. It is your primary interface.

  • AI Simulations: The terminal simulates a live cyberpunk city. You will see officers calling in traffic stops, requesting backup for rogue androids, and Tyrell Corp. security making demands.
  • Your Commands: You can type directly into the chat input bar at the bottom to send a broadcast to ALL active officers. Use 10-codes (like 10-4) for realism.
  • Furry Officers: Occasionally, genetically modified K-9 officers will ask for "head rubs". Standard officers will get annoyed, and the AI Dispatcher will issue warnings for hostility.

Secure DMs (Private Messaging)

Click the 💬 DMs tab to switch from the public broadcast frequency to end-to-end encrypted private messaging.

  • Active Roster: The sidebar shows all active units. You will notice colored status dots indicating if they are On Duty (Green), Busy (Yellow), or On Scene (Red).
  • Dynamic Sorting: If an officer messages you, they will instantly shoot to the top of the list!
  • Voice Memos & GPS: Use the 🎤 and 📍 buttons to send fake audio waves or map pings.
  • File Uploading: Click the blue + to upload a real image file from your PC and send it to an officer.
  • Self-Destruct: Type a message and click the red Bomb 💣 to send a classified message that explodes and deletes itself after 5 seconds.
  • Tapbacks: Double-click an officer's incoming message to drop a reaction emoji on it (❤️, 👍, etc).

IT Support Ticketing

The 🖥️ IT SUPPORT tab is where officers complain about broken hardware (jammed biometric scanners, blue-screening cruisers, etc).

  • Accept / Reject: You can read their issue and manually Accept or Reject the ticket.
  • Chat Reactions: Depending on your choice, the officer will react in the main terminal! If you reject them, they might curse you out, triggering the automated AI Dispatcher to suspend their comms.
  • Mass Resolve: If you have 20 tickets and no time, use the AUTO ACCEPT ALL or DENY ALL buttons. The system will sequentially reply to each one in the chat log.

Recruitment System

The 📁 JOIN MCPD tab is the HR department. You will see resumes from randomized cyberpunk citizens applying to join the force.

  • Background Checks: Read their generated history, psychological profile, and physical traits.
  • Approve/Deny: If you Approve a recruit, their name is officially added to the 5,000-unit Active Roster!
  • AI Reactions: Denied applicants will often complain in the main chat log about how unfair the system is.

Manual Event Controls

On the right-side control panel, you have manual override buttons.

  • Standard Callouts: Drop down this tab to manually force a 10-31 ROBBERY or a 10-50 TRAFFIC STOP into the terminal.
  • Department Stats: Drop down this accordion to see live-updating statistics on how many criminals have been arrested or deceased during your shift.
  • Customization: Use the color pickers at the bottom of the panel to change the UI colors of the entire website.

Log Exporting

If something hilarious or crazy happens in the chat log, you can export it as evidence.

  • Export to PDF: Generates a clean, readable PDF document of the entire chat history.
  • Export to PNG: Takes a massive, high-resolution screenshot of the chat log so you can share it.
  • System Requirement: Both buttons require you to wait for the "GENERATING..." process to finish rendering the file.
